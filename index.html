About Joseph

Passion, Precision
& Partnership

Joseph Bright is a competitive British Dressage rider and trainer based at Cork Farm in Old Wives Lees, near Chilham, between Canterbury, Faversham and Ashford in Kent. Riders come to him from right across Kent and beyond. With over 2,900 British Dressage points and a career spanning from Preliminary through to Grand Prix, Joseph brings deep technical knowledge and a genuine passion for the development of horse and rider.

Known to many as Joe, his approach is sympathetic, progressive and individually tailored, whether working with a nervous novice combination, a horse in need of retraining, or an ambitious amateur aiming for the higher levels. He produces horses from young stock through to international competition level.
