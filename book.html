Travelling a distance?

Joseph can sometimes group yard sessions together or open a quieter weekday slot for riders coming from further afield. Send the days that work and he'll see what he can do.

Wherever you ride

Live one-to-one coaching for riders across the UK, the US and beyond. Every time in the calendar is shown in your own time zone automatically.

  • FormatLive via Zoom or Pivo from your arena, or a call through your written feedback

Nothing at the right hour?

Online sessions aren't tied to yard hours. Joseph opens early mornings and evenings for riders in other time zones whenever he can. Send the times that suit you and he'll confirm a slot by reply.
